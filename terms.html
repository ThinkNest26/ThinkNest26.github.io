1. Accounts

  • Give true, up-to-date information when you sign up.

  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.
  • One person, one account. A teacher who is also a parent can use one account for both.

2. Being a good member

Think Nest is a kind, safe and respectful place. Everyone must:

  • Be kind and respectful. No bullying, unkind comments, hate or discrimination.
  • Not share private information, such as phone numbers, home addresses or passwords, theirs or anyone else’s.
  • Not post anything inappropriate, violent, frightening, or unsuitable for children.
  • Not pretend to be someone else, or try to get into another person’s account.
  • Not use Think Nest to advertise, spam or sell things without our permission.
  • Use Report if something isn’t right.

  • Think Nest is not responsible for meetups arranged by members, but we may remove any meetup.

4. What you share

  • We may remove anything that breaks these terms, and we may review reported or flagged content.

5. Teachers

  • Lesson materials made with AI tools should be checked by the teacher before students see them.
