Privacy notice

What we collect: the student’s name, email, grade, curriculum and school, a parent or guardian’s name and email, course progress, bookings, and files the student uploads.

Why: only to run the student’s Think Nest account: showing their courses, calendar, bookings and files.

Who can see it: each student can only see their own information. Think Nest staff can see it to support learning. We do not sell or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored: securely with our database provider, Supabase.

Your choices: a parent or guardian can ask us at any time to correct or delete a student’s account and data.

Contact: please use the contact details on the Think Nest website.
