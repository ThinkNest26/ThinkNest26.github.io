Privacy notice

What we collect: the student’s name, email, grade, curriculum and school; a parent or guardian’s name and email; course progress, bookings and certificates; files the student uploads; and what students share in the community, study groups and chats.

Why: only to run Think Nest: courses, calendar, mentor sessions, files and a safe space to learn together.

Who can see it: a student’s private files and progress are visible only to the student, their linked parent or guardian, their teachers and Think Nest staff. Community posts are visible to logged-in Think Nest members. Study group posts and chats are visible to the people in them and to the parents of the children in them. Think Nest staff can review content that is reported or flagged. We do not sell or share data with advertisers.

Keeping children safe: private chat is only possible between student friends who have both agreed to be friends. Adults cannot message children privately. Messages that look like they contain contact details are checked by our team.

Where it is stored: securely with our database provider, Supabase.

Your choices: a parent or guardian can ask us at any time to correct or delete a student’s account and data.

Contact: use our contact form and choose “Privacy or deleting data”.
